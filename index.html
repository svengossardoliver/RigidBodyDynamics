      WELCOME ABOARD · DECK 3 ROTATION BAY

      RIGID BODY DYNAMICS

      You're in the rotation bay of a space station orbiting Earth. The whole station is in free fall, so everything aboard is weightless, and you can study rotation without gravity getting in the way. Choose how you want to explore:

      SANDBOX · OPTION 1

      Bolted axle

      The platform turns on a frictionless axle bolted to the station frame, like a wheel on a fixed axis. Place masses, mount a thruster and fire it. Watch how moment of inertia, torque, angular acceleration and angular momentum respond. Every value is shown live.

      τ = Iα  ·  I = Σ m r²
      SANDBOX · OPTION 2

      Free-floating

      The axle is removed. With nothing holding it, the system spins about its centre of mass and drifts across the bay at the same time. See when a force causes rotation, when it causes translation, and when it causes both.

      τCM = ICMα  ·  F = Ma
      CHALLENGE

      Rescue Mission

      A debris strike has knocked out five station systems, and you're the astronaut sent to fix them. Each repair is a puzzle, harder than the last. Live values are hidden, so you have to do the calculations yourself.

      5 systems · save the station
      HOW TO USE THE BAY
      • Add a mass or thruster with the buttons in the Build panel.
      • Drag masses, the axle or the thruster to move them (1 cm snap).
      • Drag the ring at the arrow tip to aim the thruster and set its size.
      • Arrow keys nudge the selected item 1 cm (hold Shift for 10 cm).
      • Delete removes the selected mass.
      • Fire runs the burn; Pause and Reset control the run.
      Anticlockwise rotation is POSITIVE · masses in kg · grid in cm

      ⚠ MAYDAY

      INCOMING · MISSION CONTROL → SPECIALIST ON DECK 3

      A debris strike has knocked out five rotation systems on the station. Without them it will tumble, lose power and lose life support.

      You're the only crew member trained in rotational dynamics. The flight computer will give you the exact specification for each system. Rebuild it in the bay and press Check to bring it back online.

        You can try each repair as many times as you need. After three failed attempts, Mission Control can send up a worked solution, but a repair made that way doesn't count, and you'll have to fix the system again from scratch.

        STATION SAVED

        All five systems are back online. The station is stable and life support is running. Mission Control sends its thanks, astronaut.
